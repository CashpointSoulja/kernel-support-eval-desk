# Five whys

## Problem statement

The same business identity issue can be investigated more than once without becoming a durable check.

1. **Why does the issue repeat?** The useful outcome stays in an individual support conversation.
2. **Why does it stay there?** Writing a reusable case is separate from preparing the customer response.
3. **Why is that extra work easy to skip?** The input, evidence, expected behavior, and approval are captured in different places or not captured at all.
4. **Why are those details disconnected?** Support response and regression checking are treated as separate workflows.
5. **Why connect them?** A reviewed issue can supply the input and expected behavior for a small check, as long as a person approves what becomes reusable.

## Product response

The desk puts routing, cited drafting, structured data-quality capture, review, fixture export, and fixture execution in one visible loop. It does not prove that every reviewed answer is correct. It reduces the effort required to preserve an approved case.
