# Metrics

These are proposed measures for a controlled pilot. The static concept does not collect team-level reporting or claim these outcomes.

| Measure | Definition | How to read it |
|---|---|---|
| Citation validity | Share of expected citation identifiers that exist in the included corpus | The runner checks identifier existence, not whether a passage proves the answer |
| Refusal agreement | Share of fixtures where the observed refusal matches the reviewed expectation | A refusal occurs when no passage reaches the retrieval threshold |
| Route agreement | Share of fixtures whose observed route matches the reviewed route | Report by route so a strong overall result does not hide one weak category |
| Separation safety | Share of must-not-merge pairs containing two distinct identifiers | This is shape validation only, not a check against a real identity system |
| Review coverage | Share of queued items with an explicit review status | Track approval, edit, rejection, and escalation separately |
| Fixture yield | Share of approved issues exported as fixtures | A low result may signal workflow friction, not poor issue quality |
| Repeat issue coverage | Share of recurring reviewed issue types represented by at least one fixture | Requires an agreed way to group issues outside this concept |

Time saved, customer satisfaction, correction accuracy, and production defect reduction require a real pilot with approved data and baseline measurements.
